# Clipped developed-noise measurement — 8 September 2026

**The approved measurement extension passes its declared reference gates and both default RAW coverage checks.** Source measurements now share the existing generation-owned cache. Denoise and Glare still return their unchanged legacy suggestions in this commit; GPU-fitted suggestions are a separate release step.

## Implemented contract

Measurement version 3 retains the unclipped class at at most 1% endpoint pixels. The separate black-clipped class accepts more than 1% through 60% lower endpoints, with no more than the original 1% upper-endpoint allowance. Larger clipping fractions and insufficient/structured/long-correlated regions remain unsupported. Endpoints mean a pixel with any channel exactly zero or one; float headroom and negative values remain valid, and an endpoint is not claimed to identify sensor white. Class counts, accepted black fractions, and sampling/quality diagnostics are retained.

The target is centered developed clean/noisy residual variance in each domain, not pre-clipping sensor variance. MAD stays explicitly named as a robust scale. The clipped class pools variance across all absolutely qualified patches: selecting the lowest-structure quartile and taking a median selected away rare clipped excursions and understated their variance. The original unclipped selector remains unchanged except for the centered correlation/periodicity diagnostics.

For clipped components with centered lag-1 correlation above 0.3, the estimator also measures centered horizontal/vertical increments at distance 32. Beyond the declared short-range correlation support, half the increment variance estimates marginal variance while removing a planar signal's constant increment. The larger of this and local planar-residual variance avoids losing correlated noise to the fitted plane. A greater than 20% disagreement between distance-24 and distance-32 scales is classified as uncertain. This is an empirically validated short-range method, not a universal RAW correction factor.

Structure still uses **local** residual energy, so the long-separation estimate cannot disguise smooth curvature as noise. The fitted clipped structure-ratio ceiling is 0.9; the unclipped ceiling remains 0.85. Lag-8 above 0.8, periodic negative correlations below −0.15 at lags 1/4/8, and excessive highpass gain remain rejection signals. Clipped sinusoidal textures at periods 8/16/32/96 are rejected. These thresholds characterize the declared fixtures; they do not prove that arbitrary texture is statistically distinguishable from noise.

Native 64×64 patches, the 256-patch cap, independent nonoverlapping re-sampling, and four-qualified-patch minimum per populated brightness bin are preserved. No additional confidence UI, camera/ISO rule, or shader change is introduced.

## Reference audit

The extended audit contains **1,600 clean/noisy pairs**: four seeds, spatial box-filter radii 0/1/2/4/8, neutral/saturated bases, gray/unequal cross-channel covariance, five lower-clipping targets near 5/15/30/45/60%, and four actual CPU preprocessing variants. Both members of each pair receive lower clipping and the same NR/sharpening transformation. A shallow spatial plane makes the reference scenes nonconstant. Covariance, source construction, clipping selection, and preprocessing are explicit in `noise_analysis/tests/clipped.rs`.

Seeds 14951/98179 were used while selecting the method. Parameters were frozen before checking fresh seeds 73343/54787. The same bounds apply throughout: 10% or 1e-4 absolute for white-noise lower-clipped cases, 20% or 2e-4 for declared spatially correlated or CPU-preprocessed cases. Preprocessing introduces spatial correlation even when the injected noise is white. Reference residual mean bias is recorded separately and must remain part of the later GPU MSE; low variance does not mean low clipping bias.

**1,518 cases qualify and all 9,108 domain/component comparisons pass.** The other 82 cases return insufficient data under the explicit clipping/coverage/structure rules; they are not assigned zero noise or counted as successful accuracy comparisons. In particular, preprocessing can push an approximately 60%-clipped input beyond the cap, and small synthetic scenes can populate a brightness bucket with fewer than four qualified patches. Every such case is retained with rejection counts in the [case report](data/estimator-clipped-cases.csv). The full component-level JSON is generated by the command below.

The initial audit had 79 numerical failures plus 69 ineligible cases. Pooling clipped variance removed most numerical bias; the long-separation estimate addressed the remainder. Keeping the structure diagnostic local exposed and fixed a broad-curvature false positive. Final fresh-seed validation used the unchanged selected parameters. No accuracy limit was widened.

All 17 ordinary measurement tests pass, including the prior 576-comparison linear/encoded grid, 504 high-noise encoded/saturated comparisons, quantization/half-float checks, new clipped 8/16-bit pairs, explicit upper/excess-clipping rejection, texture rejection, and legacy compatibility. The two extended/local audits remain explicitly optional test runs, not skipped acceptance evidence.
